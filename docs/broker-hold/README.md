# Broker and Investor / Hold

Routes: `packages/api/src/routes/brokers.ts` (`/api/brokers`), `broker_setup.ts` (`/api/broker-setup`), `inventory_hold.ts` (`/api/inventory-hold`).
Helpers: `utils/holds.ts` (`closeHold`, `expireDueHolds`, `settleHold`), `utils/broker.ts` (`brokerOk`, `flagPaidCommission`), `utils/input.ts`, `utils/applicant.ts` (mobile, email, PAN, GSTIN).
Booking (`routes/booking.ts`) makes the commission and refuses a held unit; Surrender (`routes/surrender.ts`) cancels a pending commission. Migration `119_broker_hold.sql`.
Screens (`packages/web/public/js/pages`): `brokers.js` (Application Form, Broker List, Commission Setup, Sold-Unit Report), `broker-setup.js`, `inventory-hold.js`; menu entries "Broker" and "Investor / Hold" in `index.html`.

## Who can do what
| Action | Guard | Who gets through |
|---|---|---|
| List / read brokers, sub brokers, mapped projects | `rbac('consumers')` view | every signed-in role with consumers view (read only included) |
| PAN, GST number and bank account of a broker (detail) | same, but the fields are cut out | `super_admin`, `accounts_finance` only |
| Register a broker, edit its details, map projects | `rbac('consumers')` create / edit and process `broker_range_entry` **maker** | super_admin always; project_manager and sales_executive with the grant; accounts_finance cannot (no consumers edit) |
| Approve, reject, switch to active / inactive / suspended | `rbac('consumers')` edit and process `broker_range_entry` **checker** | super_admin; others with the grant. The person who registered a broker cannot approve it (super_admin exempt) |
| Commission list | `rbac('payments')` view | every signed-in role with payments view |
| Pay a commission, add a note | `rbac('payments')` edit and process `broker_payment_entry` **maker** | super_admin; accounts_finance and project_manager with the grant |
| Tax setup (TDS, GST) | `rbac('broker_setup')` | `super_admin`, `accounts_finance` |
| Hold list, make a hold | `rbac('inventory_hold')` view / create | super_admin, project_manager, sales_executive |
| Release a hold | same, plus owner rule | the person who made it, any project_manager, super_admin |
Project scope: a user limited to some projects (PATCH `/users/employee-login/:id` `assignedProjectIds`) only sees holds, commissions and units of those projects (database row level security): a unit or commission of another project does not exist for them (404, empty list). Brokers themselves have no project, so every user sees every broker.

## Decisions (money and state)
- **Commission** is made by the booking only: percent = `round(basic price x rate) / 100` (paise), flat = the amount; a flat commission above the unit's basic price is refused, percent above 100 is refused. Basic price = rate x carpet area, or the unit base price. A booking needs an **active** broker (pending, rejected, suspended, inactive are refused); a direct booking cannot carry a broker; a broker sale with no broker chosen (the form default) is accepted and makes no commission.
- **Project mapping** (Broker List, Projects button): a broker with ticked projects can take bookings and holds only in those (409 otherwise, also as main broker and when a booking is moved to that broker); a broker with none ticked works in every project (old data). 
- **Paying** works out GST and TDS from the broker's tax setup from the rates saved in Broker Setup for that broker. **There are no default rates:** a broker with no tax setup cannot be paid (409 "The tax setup ... is not done"), so a changed rule of law is a change in Broker Setup, never a hidden number in the code. The Tax Setup screen shows such a broker as "Not set"; the first save needs both the TDS and the GST rate: `GST = round(commission x gst%)`, `TDS = round(commission x tds%)` (to the paisa), **net paid = commission + GST - TDS**. Stored on the commission with who paid and when. A GST exempt broker gets no GST. A broker **without a PAN** is cut at least **20%** TDS (section 206AA): the higher of 20% and the setup rate.
- A commission is paid **once** (row lock; 5 parallel payments give one 200 and four 409). Only for a booking that has received its booking amount (`active` or `complete`), never for a cancelled commission, never to a suspended (or pending / rejected) broker; an inactive broker (left the firm) is still paid what was earned. A commission of 0 cannot be paid.
- A paid commission does not move: the booking's broker and commission can no longer be edited (409). Cancelling the booking after payment (allotment cancel / surrender cancellation, or booking cancel) leaves the commission **paid** (the money has gone out) and adds the note "recover it from the broker". A pending commission becomes `cancelled` when the booking is cancelled or surrendered, and cannot be paid.
- A **transfer** of the unit moves its commission (not a cancelled one) to the new holder together with the booking, so it is still payable, editable through the booking and cancelled with it. A commission now stores its `booking_id` (migration 119 fills old rows with the booking made closest in time), so a re-booking of the same unit by the same applicant never mixes two commissions. (Files outside the module that changed: `transfers.ts` one line, `booking.ts`, `surrender.ts`, `booking.js`, `booking-payment.js`: the Payment Confirmed pop-up now closes when the address changes.)
- **Hold**: only an `available` unit; the unit is locked while the hold is made; **one active hold per unit** (database index); broker must be active; 1 to 72 whole hours (the screen offers 24 and 48). Releasing frees the unit only if it is still `held`. Expired holds are closed by a job every 5 minutes **and** at once whenever holds are listed, a hold is made or the unit is booked, so a unit never stays held past its time.
- **Booking a held unit**: refused (409, "held by ...") for any other broker, a direct sale, or a broker sale without broker. The **same broker** can book it: the hold becomes `converted` and the unit goes to the booking. After the hold expired or was released anybody can book. This also covers the second booking step (payment confirmation) for a booking started before somebody held the unit.
- Suspending or deactivating a broker releases the holds it has (units become available).
- Hold history: `active`, `expired` (time up), `converted` (booked by the holder), `cancelled` (released by a person or by switching the broker off), with who and when.
- A broker is registered `pending` and needs a checker. Same mobile or PAN as another live broker (not rejected) is refused (409; a rejected broker's mobile can be used again). Only a pending broker can be approved or rejected; a rejected broker cannot be edited or switched on. A parent broker cannot be its own ancestor.
- Tax setup: TDS and GST 0 to 100 with 2 decimals; the first save needs both rates (no defaults), after that a rate left out keeps its value (it used to be reset to 5 and 18). A broker without a PAN is cut at least 20% whatever its setup says (section 206AA).

## Found and fixed here (real bugs)
High: `pageSize` of the broker and commission lists was pasted into the SQL; the hold create and release and the expiry job used `BEGIN` on the connection pool (a different connection every query, so no transaction and no lock: parallel holds and releases could interleave, and for a project limited user every statement ran alone); a unit could be held and released **once**, the second release of the same unit failed with a duplicate key (`UNIQUE (unit_id, status)`); anyone with consumers edit could **reject** any broker (even an active one) and **re-approve** a rejected one, no maker-checker on reject; commission could be set to `paid` with no check at all (any status text, repeatedly, for a cancelled commission, a pending booking, a suspended broker, and `paid_at` moved every time); TDS and GST of the broker setup were never used; a held unit could be confirmed into a booking by someone else through the two step booking flow (the unit lock overwrote the hold). After a transfer of the unit the commission stayed with the old holder (it could no longer be paid, did not follow booking edits, and was not cancelled when the new holder cancelled or surrendered).
Medium: broker register had no input checks (500 on a bad percentage, mobile, anything; commission rate sent by the screen was dropped, so every broker got 2%; bank details, city, contact, GST choice were dropped); broker setup reset the other rate to its default; commission list read fields the screen did not use (totals showed 0 / NaN-like numbers, Rate and Booking Amount empty, date filters ignored, "On Hold" status does not exist); a pending, rejected or suspended broker could take a booking and hold units; a hold had no release owner rule (anyone with hold access released anyone's hold); booking a direct sale with a broker id was accepted.
Screens: Hold screen "New Hold" tower list never filled (`towers.map is not a function`, so no hold could be made from the screen); Broker List "Deact" called a route that did not exist; the Broker List, Commission and Sold-Unit tabs showed no brokers in their pick lists when the page was opened directly on them; the GST Yes / No switch of the form was ignored; Broker Setup "Edit" said "coming soon" and the list showed only brokers that already had a row; the booking screen listed pending / rejected brokers (and only the first 100); a held unit was shown as "Booked"; the totals of Commission Setup were worked out from the first 200 rows; View showed raw HTML tags in a dialog; amounts were shown without paise.

## Not changed on purpose
- `total_referrals` is never updated (unused column).
- Commission "approved" status exists in the table but nothing sets it: payment is one step (`broker_payment_entry` has a maker only).
- No TDS threshold; GST is added on top (the company pays GST on the broker's invoice).
- A paid commission is not clawed back automatically when the booking is cancelled afterwards; it is flagged so Accounts can recover it.

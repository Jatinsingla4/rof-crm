# Captured from the client CRM, part 10: Booking Status, Parking assign, Broker detail

## Booking Status (DateWiseBookingStatus.aspx)
Filters: Project*, Payment Plan, Tower, Unit Type, Booking Status [Pending / Unapproved / Approved], Reg No, Name, Unit Address, Booking Date range. Grid columns (left to right): Booking Date, Registration No, Name, Unit Type, Unit Address, Tower, Plan Name, Received Amount, Booking Entered By + Date, Booking Unapproved By + Date, Booking Correction By + Date, Checked By + Date, Booking Approved By + Date, Booking Status, Pending To. Print and download icons. Rows seen: entered by different users (an admin, sales users, an accounts user); some bookings have Received Amount 0 (booking entered, no money yet); registration numbers are free text in mixed formats (GAL2061, "GAL 2049", 2100, 58582), so no common pattern is enforced. The approval trail is a record of who did each step and when; "Pending To" names the next person.

## Assign Car Parking (CarParkingCustomer_Assign.aspx, project with parking: ROF AMBLISS)
Filters: Project*, Unit Type, Payment Plan, Broker, Tower, Reg No, Name, Unit Address. List columns: Registration Date, Registration No, Name, Mobile, Email, Unit Type, Tower Name, Unit Address, Broker, Payment Plan, **Possession (link "Assign")**, **Parking Allotment (link)**. Buttons PRINT and DOWNLOAD.
- "Parking Allotment" opens a printable **Parking Allotment Letter** (path `/CompanyWiseLetter/Allotment/Parking_Allotment_Letter.aspx`): To, Flat/Unit No, project and address, "Sub: Allotment of Parking Space", reference to the application date and the **company**, 12 numbered terms (usage right only, no ownership; own car only; no construction or storage; cannot be sold or rented apart from the flat; company may cancel on misuse; transfers with the flat; company may change the space; allottee liable for damage; government charges borne by allottee; access for maintenance; resident sticker per car; binding on successors), a table "Types of Parking | No. of Parking | Parking No. Allotted" (example: Scooter Parking, 1, P-<tower>-<unit>), a declaration and signature blocks for the company and the first and second allottee. Letters sit in a folder per company ("CompanyWiseLetter").
- Parking slot number pattern: `P-` + tower + `-` + unit address.
- "Possession > Assign" is a separate step on the same row (marks the possession; not opened).
Ours: `parking_slots`, `parking_types`, `parking_prices`, Parking tabs (type master, inventory, assign, inventory report). To check: a printable parking allotment letter with these 12 terms and the slot number pattern.

## Broker details (this login sees only the broker "Direct", code ROF1)
Broker List columns: S.No, Broker Code, Company Name, Bank Name, Name, DOB, Service Tax No, GSTIN, GST State, Effective Date, RERA No, PAN, TAN, Licence No, STD Code, Phone, Mobile, Email, Address, Detail, Edit, **Continue / Dis-Continue**, Delete. Filters: Broker Company Name, Broker Name.
Broker Details report (ReportBrokerDetail.aspx): Parent Broker Company, Company Name, Broker Name, Address, Phone No, Mobile No, E-Mail, TAN No, PAN No, Is Service Tax applicable?, Service Tax No, GSTIN, GST State Code, GST Effective Date, RERA No, Reference, Deposit Money, Remark. (Parent Broker Company = the sub-broker link.) "Direct" is a real broker record (code ROF1, GST Unregistered from 01 Jul 2017), used when no broker is involved.
Ours: `brokers`, `broker_applications`, `broker_project_mapping`, `broker_tax_config`; screens Broker List, Application Form, Commission Setup, Broker Setup (Tax Masters). To check: "Dis-Continue / Continue" switch, Parent Broker on the form, a printable Details report, and a built-in "Direct" broker.

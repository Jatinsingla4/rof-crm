# Captured from the client CRM, part 3: what each list/report shows (column headers only, no customer data)
Method: each screen was fetched once for project "ROF GALLERIA@78" and only the header row was kept. Screens that showed "No record found" for that project have no columns here (they need a project with data, or a dummy project). Every report screen also has the buttons Fetch and "Chart Report (Alt+H)".

## Sale flow
- **Customer Register** (PaymentPlanReport_All): S.No, Registration Date, Registration No, Name, Mobile, Email, Unit Type, Tower, Unit Address, Employee, Broker, Payment Plan, Customer Classification, Area, Rate, Average Rate, Basic Price, Discount, Net Basic Price, PLC, Other Charge, IFMS, Total Unit Cost, Received Amount, Interest, Admin Charge, Status, Finance Type, Bank Name, Customer Status. Row buttons: **Lock, Unlock, Print Welcome, SMS, E-Mail** (bulk, on selected rows).
- **Booking Status** (DateWiseBookingStatus): Booking Date, Reg No, Name, Unit Type, Unit Address, Tower, Plan, Received Amount, Booking Entered By/Date, Booking Unapproved By/Date, Booking Correction By/Date, Checked By/Date, Booking Approved By/Date, Booking Status, Pending To. (A 3-step maker/checker/approver trail.)
- **Editing Status**: same idea for edits of an applicant (Pending for approval / Unapproved / Approved / Rejected).
- **Allotment Form** list: Registration Date, Reg No, Name, Unit Type, Unit Address, Broker, Photo, Allotment (action).
- **Allotment Letter** list: Reg No, First and Second Applicant, Unit Address, Broker Company, Unit Type, Area, Rate, Basic Price, Allotment Date, Print By, Print Date, Print Status, Allotment Letter Type 1, Site Map.
- **Allotment and Agreement Status**: Reg No, Name, Broker, Tower, Unit Address, Area, Allotment Status, Agreement Status.
- **Receipt Print**: Receipt (link), Reg No, Name, Tower, Unit No, Receipt Date, Receipt No, Mode of Payment, Net Amount, Generated By/Date, Printed By/Date, Receipt Type, Approval Status, Audit Trail.
- **Cancel Receipt Report**: Reg No, Name, Payment Type, Payment Date, Amount, Receipt No, Unit Type, Receipt Remarks, Cancelled By/Date.
- **TDS Challan Report**: Reg No, Name, Unit No, Receipt Date, Receipt No, Payment Type, Amount, Challan Date, Challan No, TDS Status, Certificate No, Bank Name, Certificate Document, Challan Document, Remark.
- **Print Demand / Demand Status**: Reg No, Booking Date, Name, Broker, Type, Unit Address, Tower, Floor, Mobile, Phone, Email, Plan, Installment No, Installment Date, **Delay days**, Area, Rate, Basic Price, Total Unit Cost, Due Amount, CGST, SGST, Paid Amount, O/S Amount, Interest Amount, O/S With Interest, Pending Cheque, Location. Buttons: D / R1 / R2 (select letter type), Print Demand, Print Reminder 1, Print Reminder 2.
- **Surrender / Cancellation Report** (about 50 columns): registration, applicant, address, tower, floor, unit, plan, broker, team, classification, Date of Cancellation, area, rate, total unit cost, other charge with / without agreement, PLC, IFMS, Lessee Rent, total sale value, sale value with tax, paid by applicant (amount, tax, CGST, SGST, ITC, gross), penalty, premium, payable by company, paid, balance, remark, surrender by, final surrender date and remark, type, status.
- **Broker View/Edit** list: Broker Code, Company, Bank, Name, DOB, Service Tax No, GSTIN, GST State, Effective Date, RERA, PAN, TAN, Licence, STD, Phone, Mobile, Email, Address, buttons Detail / Edit / Continue / Dis-Continue / Delete.
- Broker reports: Sold-Unit (Broker Code, Name, Company, Sold Unit, Detail), Broker Wise Booking (Broker, Project, No. of Booking), Broker Corporate (Broker Company, Total, Retail), Summary, Broker Dues/BSP, Broker Hierarchy (tree).

## Inventory
- **Unit Status** (project wise): Reg No, Name, Tower, Floor, Group, Unit Type, Area, Carpet Area, Unit No, Location, Broker, Status; row button Hold.
- Unit Wise PLC: Tower, Unit Address, Unit Size, Unit Type, Status. Sold/Available Area: Unit Type, Area, Total Unit, Total Area, Increase Area, Total with Increase, Sold Unit/Area, Balance Unit/Area. Tower vs Unit Type: matrix, columns = unit sizes.
- **Unit Wise Area** (editable, <Save>): Reg No, Name, Tower, Floor, Unit No, Area Measurement, Super, Built-up, Carpet, UDSL, Plot, **Balcony 1, 2, 3, Basement, Stilt, Common** areas. Report has the same columns.

## Applicant
- Applicant Payment File: customer details, cost, dues, received, credit, outstanding, interest (grouped columns). Customers Names (All Applicants): Reg No, Address, Unit Type, 1st, 2nd, **3rd** Applicant.

## Collection
- Collection Report: Project, Clear, Pending (Under Clearing), Received.
- **Receipt Wise Collection** (30 columns): Reg No, Name, Registration Date, Unit No, Team, Classification, Received From, Receipt Date, Receipt No, Amount, CGST, SGST, Net, Type, Cheque No, Bank, Branch, Cheque Date, Status, Clear/Bounce Date, Clear Bank, Deposited Date, Deposited Bank, On Account Of, Remark, Receipt Status, Cancel Remark, Created By/Date.
- Customer Wise Balance: Reg No, Booking Date, Name, Unit Type, Unit Address, Broker, Rate, Unit Area, Opening (incl. S.Tax), CR, DR, Closing (incl. S.Tax), Closing, Closing Service Tax, Status.
- Head Wise vertical: Reg No, Booking Date, Name, Tower, Unit No, Head Name, Amount, CGST, SGST, Total. Horizontal: customer, BSP amount, one column per head, total.

## Dues
- Customer Wise Dues: customer details, dues, received, outstanding, undue. Customer Dues Details: customer, dues, received, outstanding, interest.
- Due Report (project): No. of Customers, Total Cost (A), Due (B), Due %, Pending (C), Clear (D), Received (E = C + D), Credit, Outstanding, Received % (E*100/A), Outstanding % (G*100/B), Details.
- Tower Wise Stage Report: one column per payment stage (At the time of booking, On Allotment, Within 30 Days of Booking, On the Start of Excavation, On Casting of Ground Floor Slab, On Completion of Super Structure, On Intimation for Offer of Possession ...) + Total. Plan and Stage wise: Stage, No. of Customers, CLP, Received, Credit, Outstanding. Percentage Wise: Dues in (%), No. of Customers, Due, Paid, Outstanding.
- **Due Report With All Charges (%)**: Reg No, Booking Date, Name, Tower, Unit No, Unit Type, Area, Broker, Plan, Loan, Rate, Basic, PLC, Other Charges, IFMS, Unit Cost, Add On, Total Consideration, Due, Due %, Received, Received %, OS, OS %, Balance, Balance %.
- Charge detail: Parking (Reg No, Name, Unit, Total Charge, Total Amount), Customer PLC (Reg No, Name, Booking Date, Unit, Total Amount), Customer Alteration (Reg No, Name, Booking Date, Unit).
- View Payment Plan: Installment Name, BSP (percent of price).
